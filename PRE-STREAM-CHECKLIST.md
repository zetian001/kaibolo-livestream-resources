# Pre-stream checklist · 开播前检查清单

[English resources](https://kaibolo.com/en/xue/) · [中文学习资料](https://kaibolo.com/xue/)

Use this checklist for a normal creator livestream. Add any checks specific to your platform, format and team.

## English

### Purpose and content

- [ ] Write the purpose of this session in one sentence.
- [ ] Prepare a short introduction: who you are, what the stream offers and how viewers can join in.
- [ ] List three talking points or segments, with a backup topic for quiet moments.
- [ ] Choose one audience question or activity that newcomers can understand immediately.
- [ ] Prepare a clear closing and the topic of the next session.

### Picture and sound

- [ ] Check framing, background and lighting using the actual streaming camera.
- [ ] Record and listen to a short microphone test; check clarity, clipping and background noise.
- [ ] Confirm that music or other audio does not overpower your voice.
- [ ] Check the network, power supply and remaining battery where relevant.
- [ ] Close unrelated windows and hide private notifications before sharing a screen.
- [ ] Test scenes, overlays and any planned transitions.

### Session operations

- [ ] Check the title, category and cover against the content you will actually deliver.
- [ ] Review the current platform requirements that apply to your planned content and materials.
- [ ] If a moderator is involved, agree on responsibilities and how to flag a problem.
- [ ] Prepare a simple recovery plan for audio, network or software interruptions.
- [ ] Decide which session metrics you will record, using the platform's own definitions.

### Final check

- [ ] Preview the stream from a viewer's perspective where possible.
- [ ] Keep water and essential notes within reach.
- [ ] Open the [review template](STREAM-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md) for use after the session.

## 中文

### 目标与内容

- [ ] 用一句话写清这一场直播的目标。
- [ ] 准备简短自我介绍：你是谁、直播提供什么、观众怎样参与。
- [ ] 列出三个话题或环节，并准备冷场时的备用话题。
- [ ] 准备一个新观众能立即理解的提问或互动。
- [ ] 准备明确的结束语和下一场的主题。

### 画面与声音

- [ ] 用实际直播摄像头检查构图、背景和灯光。
- [ ] 录一段麦克风测试并回听，检查清晰度、爆音和环境噪声。
- [ ] 确认背景音乐等声音没有盖过人声。
- [ ] 检查网络、电源；需要时检查剩余电量。
- [ ] 分享屏幕前关闭无关窗口，隐藏私人通知。
- [ ] 测试场景、画面叠加元素和计划使用的切换。

### 直播执行

- [ ] 确认标题、分类和封面与实际直播内容一致。
- [ ] 核对本场内容和素材适用的最新平台要求。
- [ ] 有场控时，明确职责和问题提醒方式。
- [ ] 为声音、网络或软件中断准备简单的恢复方案。
- [ ] 按平台自身的指标口径，确定下播后要记录的数据。

### 最后确认

- [ ] 条件允许时，从观众端预览画面和声音。
- [ ] 把水和必要提示放在手边。
- [ ] 打开[复盘模板](STREAM-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md)，方便下播后填写。
