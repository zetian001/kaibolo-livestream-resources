# Contributing · 参与完善

Useful contributions include clearer wording, translation corrections, broken-link reports and practical additions to the templates.

For a website correction, include:

1. The page URL and the relevant passage.
2. The specific problem and your suggested wording.
3. An official source link and publication date if the change concerns platform requirements or news.

For a template addition, explain when the check is useful and keep the wording short enough to use during a real session. Keep personal details, account credentials and private audience records out of public issues and examples.

## 中文

欢迎补充更清楚的表述、翻译修正、失效链接，以及模板中的实用检查项。

反馈网站内容时，请提供：

1. 页面链接和对应段落。
2. 具体问题与建议改法。
3. 涉及平台要求或新闻时，附官方来源链接和发布日期。

补充模板时，请说明适用场景，尽量写成真实直播中能快速使用的一句话。公开问题和示例中不要放个人隐私、账号凭据或观众的私人记录。
