# Kaibolo · Practical Livestreaming Resources

[English website](https://kaibolo.com/en/) · [中文网站](https://kaibolo.com/) · [中文说明](README.zh-CN.md)

Kaibolo (开播咯) is a streamer learning resource for creators, livestream operators and agency teams. It brings together beginner training, conversation prompts, stream planning, audience interaction, equipment setup and industry updates. Its English edition is now available alongside the Chinese website.

This repository provides two practical templates to accompany the learning materials: a pre-stream checklist and a stream review form. Start with a specific preparation or performance problem, use the relevant guide, then record what changed in your next session.

Many platform guides and news articles concern the Chinese livestreaming ecosystem. The English edition makes those materials easier to access for international readers.

## Use the templates

1. Work through the [pre-stream checklist](PRE-STREAM-CHECKLIST.md) before going live.
2. Fill in the [stream review template](STREAM-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md) after the session.
3. Choose one change to test next time and compare the results.

Both templates include English and Chinese sections.
